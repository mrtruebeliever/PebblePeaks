# Pebble Peaks — Store Listing

## Short Description
Race up a mountain while the mist rises. Jump, upgrade, survive, and reach the 1000m summit.

## Full Description

Pebble Peaks is a vertical climber where every second counts. Spring from ledge to ledge up an endless alpine face while a rising mist closes in from below. One false jump and you fall—or the cold catches you.

### Gameplay
- **Jump left or right** to reach the next ledge
- **Collect gems** scattered across the mountain to spend in the shop
- **Unlock upgrades**: Grip Soles for better ice control, Down Jacket to slow the mist, Climbing Rope for emergency rescue
- **Reach 1000 meters** and plant your flag at the summit

### Challenges
- **Ice ledges** slide you toward the edge—jump before you fall off
- **Crumble ledges** vanish seconds after landing
- **Falling rocks** knock you down to lower ground
- **Rising mist** ends your run if it reaches you

### Features
- Procedurally generated mountain with parallax peaks and realistic snow
- Physics-verified jump system—reachability proven over thousands of layouts
- 5 languages: English, Dutch, French, German, Spanish
- Persistent gem wallet, height records, and achievement tracking
- Compact build (24 KB) for the Pebble Time 2

### Who's This For?
Arcade fans who love quick, skill-based challenges. Similar vibe to Icy Tower or Doodle Jump, but with alpine hazards and persistent progression.
