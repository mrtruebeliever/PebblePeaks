# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- **First release.** A vertical climber for the Pebble Time 2: UP jumps
  up-left, DOWN jumps up-right along a fixed arc, no steering mid-air. Climb
  from ledge to ledge while freezing mist rises from below.
- **Ledges with character.** Snow-capped rock, slippery ice with icicles
  (you slide toward the edge) and cracked crumble ledges that tremble, sag
  and give way, then grow back a few seconds later. Falling rocks from 300 m.
- **Gems and a shop.** Gems carry over between runs. Spend them on Grip
  soles, a Down jacket and a Climbing rope, five levels each.
- **Grappling hook.** With rope left, SELECT mid-air fires the hook at a
  ledge near the middle of the screen and the climber climbs up hand over
  hand. SELECT on a ledge raises the hook instead: tilt the watch to aim,
  SELECT fires, BACK lowers it. Two aiming styles, switched with UP on the
  pause screen: a swinging aim line (timing) or tilting the watch. Every so
  often a gem ledge hangs out of jump reach, only for climbers with rope
  left; it holds two gems and a rope coil, so there's always a rope to get
  back off it. Rare rope coils also turn up on ordinary ledges (up to 9
  ropes carried). A missed shot costs the rope.
- **The summit.** At 1000 m the mountain ends in a flag. Reach it with full
  gear, walk over and grab it; the flag then stands beside your climber on
  the title screen.
- **16-bit look.** Pixel-art climber with jump, fall, landing and rope
  climbing poses, sparkling gems and tumbling boulders, over a parallax
  background: a pine valley and snowy ranges that sink away below, drifting
  clouds, and a sky that runs from day through sunset to a starry night with
  an aurora near the summit.
- **Chip sounds.** Gems, the hook's throw and clank, rocks,
  crackling ledges, a mist warning and jingles for a record and the summit,
  on the Time 2 speaker. Sound level Off/Soft/Normal/Loud on the pause
  screen (DOWN); Quiet Time and the watch's mute always win.
- **Tinted backlight.** While the light is on it takes the sky's colour,
  pulses red when the mist is close, and glows gold on the summit.
- **Five languages:** English, Nederlands, Français, Deutsch, Español,
  following the watch's language.
